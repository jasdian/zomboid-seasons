Scoring System

Your season score is the sum of three components from your best character plus your online consistency:

ComponentFormulaMax
Online10 pts per day online (30min+)~900
KillsDiminishing returns (cap: 1000 kills)~497
SurvivalExponential (cap: 30 game-days)700

At season end, your score is credited to your permanent account balance. Top 3 scorers get free registration for the next season.
